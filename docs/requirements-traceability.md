# Requirements traceability — current evidence and gaps

**Date:** 2026-10-30
**Scope:** current working tree; the historical product goals are in [PROJECT_SPEC.md](../PROJECT_SPEC.md). This is not release certification. A passing unit test establishes only the tested behavior, not a complete requirement or security guarantee.

| ID | Original goal | Current state and evidence | Remaining gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-01 | Local-first storage and atomic project/episode changes | **Partial.** SQLite transactions, scoped IDs, revisions and allocation are tested; an offline metadata-plus-files bundle and isolated staged restore are exercised in `server/backupRecovery.test.ts`. | Complete nested schemas/legacy imports, hostile filesystem races, independent backup operations, full-size recovery rehearsal and live promotion/rollback remain open. |
| REQ-02 | Durable job states and external-operation recovery | **Partial.** Local supplied-asset render claims, failures, cancellation and segment retry have isolated tests in `server/localMedia.test.ts`; `server/localMediaReliability.test.ts` covers conservative same-domain post-start recovery and identity ambiguity. | No autonomous provider workflow or distributed lease. Live/reused PIDs, foreign/unknown domains and legacy claims are not automatically reclaimed; non-Linux cross-process recovery, orphan children and cleanup/restore scenarios remain incomplete. |
| REQ-03 | Scoped spending approval and budget enforcement | **Partial / execution disabled.** Internal ledger cap and transaction tests use synthetic fixture grants; see `server/auditRemediation.test.ts`. | Grant creation and paid dispatch/provider integration are disabled; no independent human identity or financial authorization is established. |
| REQ-04 | Original Ternfall series, arcs and 1,000 episodes | **Partial.** The original draft and scoped roadmap exist; arc expansion produces draft cards. `src/core/storyCraft.test.ts` and `server/arcExpansion.test.ts` cover bounded behavior. | A roadmap or generated card is not an authored, reviewed or produced episode. Scope and continuity need human review. |
| REQ-05 | Factual research, SSRF and injection defenses | **Partial.** Real-world notes are unverified; bounded URL syntax/literal-host and text-pattern checks are advisory (`src/core/sources.test.ts`, `server/researchEdits.test.ts`). | No source fetching or factual certification; DNS, redirects and all reserved addresses are not validated; pattern checks are not prompt-injection protection. |
| REQ-06 | Word counts, pacing and distinctness | **Partial.** Deterministic estimates and advisory heuristics are tested in `src/core/rules.test.ts` and `src/core/storyCraft.test.ts`. | Estimates are not measured narration duration; similarity/keyword checks do not certify quality, originality or canon. |
| REQ-07 | Storyboarding, limited animation, multistem mixing and captions | **Not met.** Some planning and caption-format helpers exist. | No complete animated storyboard, voice/Foley generator, multistem mixing workflow, caption synchronization or Shorts production. |
| REQ-08 | Honest runtime diagnostics and secret protection | **Partial.** Doctor probes configured binaries; exercised error paths suppress unexpected details. Local-render, project and settings HTTP success responses use scoped projections (`server/localMediaApiSafety.test.ts`, `server/projectApiPrivacy.test.ts`, `server/settingsApiPrivacy.test.ts`). | Other success responses and untested paths still need privacy review; intended user-visible text may contain sensitive content. No provider connectivity or GPU benchmarking, complete credential redaction, hardened deployment or production-readiness certification. |
| REQ-09 | YouTube upload/handoff, privacy and disclosure | **Not implemented.** Historical data is viewable but production metadata packages and platform operations are retired/disabled. | No authenticated upload, private handoff, rights review or public release workflow; no audience-performance or cost claims are verified. |
| REQ-10 | Resumable end-to-end autoproducer | **Disabled.** Legacy paths return 501 or fail closed; `server/security.test.ts` and `src/core/flow.test.ts` cover representative guards. | Provider integrations, durable independent approvals and full evidence/recovery would be required before consideration. |
| REQ-11 | Real local video output | **Partial.** Supplied PNG/audio rendering, actual media QC, hash-checked playback and segment retry have integration tests in `server/localMedia.test.ts`. | No asset generation, editorial/rights review or assurance for native binaries, hostile concurrent mutation, all platforms or resource extremes. The 900-second test is opt-in. |
| REQ-12 | Safe, useful agent guidance | **Partial.** Twenty project-specific and 12 pinned upstream OpenCode skills are catalogued in [the skill README](../.opencode/skills/README.md); isolated OpenCode 1.19.5 discovered and individually loaded all 32. | Instructions and permission globs are not a sandbox or grant. No claim that all internet skills were found, that a model will obey them, or that other runtimes were validated. |

See [fresh research audit](research-audit-and-remediation-2026-10-30.md), [independent baseline audit](independent-audit-2026-10-30.md) and [remediation progress](remediation-progress.md) for evidence, validation and outstanding risk.
